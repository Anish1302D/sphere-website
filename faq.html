Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About Sphere

Whether you're a beginner exploring technology or an experienced developer looking to build impactful projects, we're here to help you grow.

Who can join Sphere Community?

expand_more
Sphere is open to students from B.Sc Computer Science, Data Science, Cyber Security, and Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning at SP College. Students with a genuine passion for technology and innovation are encouraged to apply.

Do I need prior coding experience?

expand_more
Not at all. Beginners are welcome. We provide learning opportunities, mentorship, and hands-on projects to help members build their skills from the ground up.

Is there any membership fee?

expand_more
No. Membership is completely free. Sphere is committed to making learning, collaboration, and innovation accessible to every student.

What activities does Sphere organize?

expand_more

We regularly conduct:

  • Technical Workshops
  • Hackathons
  • Project Development
  • Open Source Contributions
  • Guest Sessions
  • Coding Challenges
  • Networking Events
  • Tech Talks

Can I participate if I'm from another department?

expand_more
Our primary focus is students from Computer Science, Data Science, Cyber Security, and AI/ML. Students from other disciplines may be considered for collaborations depending on club activities.

How are members selected?

expand_more
Selection is based on your interest, motivation, willingness to learn, teamwork, and participation in the recruitment process. Prior experience is an advantage but not a requirement.

What technologies will I learn?

expand_more

Members explore a wide range of technologies including:

Artificial Intelligence Full-Stack Development Cyber Security Data Science Cloud Computing DevOps Mobile App Development UI/UX Design Open Source

Do I have to participate in hackathons?

expand_more
Hackathons are highly encouraged because they provide valuable real-world experience, but participation is optional.

Can I build my own project?

expand_more
Absolutely. Members are encouraged to propose their own ideas, form teams, and receive guidance from peers and mentors to turn concepts into working products.

Will I receive certificates?

expand_more
Certificates may be provided for selected workshops, events, or competitions, depending on the nature of the activity.

How can I stay updated?

expand_more
Follow Sphere's official communication channels, including WhatsApp, Discord, and the club website, for announcements, events, and opportunities.

How do I join?

expand_more
Complete the recruitment form during the registration period. Shortlisted candidates will be contacted with the next steps.

Still Have Questions?

We'd love to hear from you. Drop us an email or apply directly to join the club.
